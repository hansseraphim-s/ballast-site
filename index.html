The premise

Ballast is the weight below the waterline that keeps a ship upright when everything above it is moving.

Enterprise AI has plenty of sail. This show is about the weight: the budgets, the infrastructure that makes an enterprise run, and now the AI controls and governance that decide whether any of it holds.
