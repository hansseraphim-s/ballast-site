Privacy

  • The guest and newsletter forms send what you type to Google (Apps Script, Google Sheets and Gmail), which logs it, emails you a confirmation and notifies the host.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when the page loads.
  • Once enabled, the site uses Vercel Web Analytics, which Vercel documents as cookieless.
  • No ads and no tracking cookies.
